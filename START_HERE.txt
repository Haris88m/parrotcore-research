IMPORTANT
---------
13.6A is the theoretical all-servo stall current from manufacturer data.
The 15A servo regulator has little sustained-stall margin, so 12A is the current
simulator fault threshold and all-servo stall is prohibited as an operating mode.

There are NO physical V1 measurements yet. Dynamic current, runtime, thermal
behavior, total mass, center of gravity and torque adequacy remain models.
