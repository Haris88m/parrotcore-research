# Product Vision — Embodied Intelligence Platform

## Product thesis

ParrotCore is not intended to be a novelty animatronic. The target is a **reusable embodied-intelligence platform** whose AI identity and skills can persist across different expressive physical bodies.

## What the platform should ultimately achieve

A production system should be able to:

- detect and orient toward a person;
- understand local speech;
- answer with local or optional cloud language models;
- synchronize beak/mouth motion with speech;
- blink, gesture and shift posture naturally;
- remember explicitly permitted facts and learned associations;
- recognize selected people and objects;
- teach and practice lessons;
- learn approved new trigger/response or skill packages;
- expose a constrained developer SDK;
- survive AI-process failure by placing actuator safety on the MCU;
- move the same cognitive identity into a different compatible creature body.

## Reference product roles

### Parrot Companion
**Primary strength:** speech feels natural for the animal metaphor.  
**Target use:** family interaction, storytelling, language practice, companionship, demonstrations.

### Owl Tutor
**Primary strength:** large head/eyes and a wide head-yaw envelope support attention and teaching gestures.  
**Target use:** classroom, reading practice, quizzes, guided learning.

### Raven Dev
**Primary strength:** sharper motion language and a deliberately technical identity.  
**Target use:** developers, robotics labs, AI demonstrations, research prototypes.

## What v0.4 proves

v0.4 does **not** prove market demand, physical reliability, motor life, acoustic performance, battery endurance or child safety.

It does prove at software/simulation level that:

1. three different bodies can load under one registry;
2. they can use different actuator names and ranges;
3. one semantic command can compile into different motions;
4. safety limits still apply at execution;
5. each body has a conceptual 3D kinematic twin;
6. the old v0.3 runtime contract remains functional.

That is the foundation needed before perception and local AI are attached.
